Here’s how to invite an external guest to a **single board** as a **read-only viewer**:

### Steps to Invite an External Viewer to One Board:
1. **Go to the specific board** you want to share (e.g., “Marketing Assets”).
2. Click on the **Share** button (usually in the top-right corner or in the sidebar, depending on your plan/interface).
3. In the share dialog, choose **“Invite by email”** or paste the guest’s email address.
4. Before sending, look for the **role/permission selector** and choose **“Viewer”** (not Editor or Member).
5. **Important:** Make sure the option **“Give access to this board only”** or **“Limit access to this board”** is selected.  
   - *Note:* The exact wording may vary by workspace type (e.g., Trello, Asana, Monday.com, etc.), but most platforms offer a way to restrict guest access to a single item/board.
6. Send the invite. The guest will receive an email with a link to sign in (or create an account) and will only see that one board.

### Key Benefits:
- ✅ **Read-only**: They can view tasks, cards, comments (if allowed), and attachments but cannot edit or add content.
- ✅ **Single-board scope**: They won’t see other boards or sensitive internal projects.
- ✅ **No paid seat required**: Most platforms allow unlimited (or a generous number) of free guest/viewer accounts.

### Pro Tips:
- If your platform doesn’t allow board-level permission control for guests, check if you can create a **public share link** with viewer-only permissions instead.
- For added security, enable **guest expiration** if available, so access automatically ends when the contract does.
