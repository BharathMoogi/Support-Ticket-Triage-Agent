Here are the two easiest ways to export your Q1 Roadmap board to Excel or CSV:

### Method 1: Quick Export (Best for preserving board structure)
This method exports your data in a format that respects your columns (statuses) and rows, which is often ideal for reports.

1.  Open your **Q1 Roadmap** board.
2.  Click the **More** icon (three dots `…`) in the top-right corner of the screen.
3.  Select **Print or export**.
4.  Choose **Download as CSV** or **Download as Excel**.
    *   *Note:* This will include all cards, their current status (column), labels, and description fields.

### Method 2: Card List View (Best for detailed field data like Assignees & Due Dates)
If Method 1 doesn’t include the specific **Assignee** or **Due Date** columns you need in the way you want them, this is the most robust option:

1.  From the board view, click the **List** tab (next to Board/Kanban) at the top.
2.  Look for **Custom Fields** or **Columns** to ensure "Assignee" and "Due Date" are visible if needed (though they are usually included by default in exports).
3.  Click the **Export** button (usually found in the top-right corner of the List view) or go to **More (…）** > **Print or export** > **Export list as CSV**.
4.  In the export dialog, make sure to check:
    *   ✅ **Due Date**
    *   ✅ **Assignee**
    *   ✅ **Status**
    *   ✅ **Card Title**
    *   ✅ **Labels** (if applicable)
5.  Click **Export**.

### Tips for Your Executive Report:
*   **CSV vs. Excel:** CSV works universally but Excel (.xlsx) preserves formatting better if you plan to use formulas or charts.
*   **Date Format:** Due dates typically export as YYYY-MM-DD. You may need to format the date column in Excel for a cleaner look (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Assignee Names:** Assignees will export as their full name or email address, depending on your workspace settings. If you only see emails, know that they correspond to your team members directly.
