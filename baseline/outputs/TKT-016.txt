Thank you for upgrading to the Team plan! I’d be happy to help you get Okta SAML SSO set up for your team.

However, because these values are **unique to your specific workspace**, I cannot provide a universal `ACS URL` or `Entity ID`. These are generated when you first initialize the SAML connection in our application.

Here is exactly where to find them and how to complete the setup:

### Step 1: Generate Values in Our App
1. Log in to your workspace as an **Admin**.
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **Security** (or **Authentication**, depending on your UI version).
3. Click on **SSO / SAML Setup**.
4. Select **Okta** (or **Manual SAML**) as your IdP.
5. You will see two key values automatically generated:
   - **ACS URL** (also called Assertion Consumer Service URL)
   - **Entity ID** (also called SP Entity ID)

> 🔑 **Copy these two values from this screen.** These are the only values you need to paste into Okta.

### Step 2: Configure in Okta
In your **Okta Admin Console**:
1. Go to **Applications** > **Create App Integration** > **SAML 2.0**.
2. Paste our **Entity ID** into the **Audience URI (SP Entity ID)** field.
3. Paste our **ACS URL** into the **ACS URL** field.
4. Set **NameID Format** to `Persistent` (most common) or `Email Address` if preferred.
5. Save the app. Okta will generate its own **Identity Provider (IdP) data**, including:
   - **SAML 2.0 Issuer**
   - **SAML 2.0 Certificate (X.509)**
   - **Single Sign-On URL (SSO URL)**

### Step 3: Paste Okta’s Certificate into Our App
1. Return to our **Settings > Security > SSO** page.
2. You will see a field labeled **IdP Metadata**, **X.509 Certificate**, or **Certificate Public Key**.
3. **Paste Okta’s X.509 Certificate here.**  
   > 📌 *Tip: If our app asks for "Issuer" or "SSO URL," paste those from Okta’s IdP data as well.*
4. Click **Save** or **Complete Setup**.

### Step 4: Test
1. After saving, our app will send a test assertion to Okta.
2. Try logging out and logging back in using Okta.

### Need Exact Values?
If you’re already on the SSO setup page and want to confirm the format, you can:

- Or, **log out/in** to see if the SSO prompt appears after setup.
