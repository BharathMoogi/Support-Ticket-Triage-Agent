# Payment Methods, Invoices & Tax IDs

## Accepted Payment Methods
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
- **PayPal**: Available for all monthly and annual Pro/Team plans.
- **ACH / Wire Transfer / Purchase Orders**: Available exclusively for **Team plans** on annual billing contracts exceeding ,000/year.

## Downloading Invoices
1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing > Invoice History**.
2. Click **Download PDF** next to any billing period.

## Adding VAT, GST, or Tax IDs
To add your company tax registration number to past or future invoices:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Billing Details**.
2. Enter your **VAT / Tax ID** and legal company address.
3. Click **Save & Update Invoices**. All previously generated PDF receipts will automatically update with the VAT ID.
