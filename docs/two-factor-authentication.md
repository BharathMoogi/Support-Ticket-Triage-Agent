# Setting Up and Troubleshooting Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

## Enabling 2FA
1. Go to **Profile Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code using an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy).
4. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code.
5. **Download your 8 Backup Recovery Codes** and store them safely.

## Troubleshooting Invalid 2FA Codes
- **Time Synchronization**: TOTP codes require accurate device clocks. On Android, go to Authenticator **Settings > Time correction for codes > Sync now**. On iOS, check **Settings > General > Date & Time > Set Automatically**.
- **Using Backup Codes**: If your phone is lost, click **Use Backup Code** at login.
- **Account Lockout**: If you have lost both your authenticator and backup codes, open a support ticket from your registered account email for identity verification.
