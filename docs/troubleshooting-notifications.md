# Troubleshooting Notifications & Alert Emails

If you are missing email notifications, browser push alerts, or in-app bell notifications:

## In-App Notification Bell
- Check that you are directly assigned to the card or listed as a **Watcher**. Unwatched cards do not generate activity feed alerts.
- Verify your settings in **Profile > Preferences > Notification Frequency** (Instant vs Daily Digest).

## Email Notifications
1. **Check Spam / Quarantine**: Ensure emails from otifications@flowboard.app\ are not filtered.
2. **Digest Delay**: If set to *Hourly* or *Daily Digest*, individual card changes are bundled and sent on schedule rather than immediately.
3. **Muted Boards**: Check if the board is muted under **Board Menu (...) > Mute Board Activity**.

## Desktop / Browser Push Notifications
1. Click the lock/tune icon next to the browser URL bar (\lowboard.app\).
2. Ensure **Notifications** permission is set to **Allow**.
3. On macOS, verify that **System Settings > Notifications > [Your Browser]** has notifications enabled and Focus Mode / Do Not Disturb is off.
