# Guest Access & External Sharing

## Single-Board Guests
Invite external clients, freelancers, or contractors to specific boards without granting access to the broader workspace.
- **Inviting a Guest**: Open the board, click **Share > Invite**, enter the email, and choose **Guest (Single Board)**.
- **Permissions**: Guests can be set to **Can Edit** or **Can View (Read-Only)**.
- **Pricing**: Free and unlimited read-only viewers on Pro and Team plans. Editing guests consume a guest license.
