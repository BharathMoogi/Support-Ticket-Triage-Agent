# FlowBoard Plan Limits & Pricing

FlowBoard offers three tiers designed to scale with your team:

| Feature | Free Tier | Pro Tier (/user/mo) | Team Tier (/user/mo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Boards** | Up to 3 boards | Unlimited boards | Unlimited boards |
| **Team Members** | Up to 5 members | Unlimited members | Unlimited members |
| **File Attachment Limit** | 10 MB per file | 250 MB per file | 1 GB per file |
| **Total Workspace Storage** | 100 MB | 10 GB per user | Unlimited |
| **Automation Runs** | 100 runs/month | 1,000 runs/user/month | 10,000 runs/user/month |
| **Custom Fields** | 1 per board | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **SAML SSO & Audit Logs** | Not available | Not available | Included |
| **Support SLA** | Community / 72h | Standard email (<24h) | Priority (<4h) |

*Note: Billing is per active user per month. Inactive or deactivated members are not billed.*
