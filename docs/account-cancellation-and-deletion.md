# Account Cancellation and Workspace Deletion

## Cancelling a Paid Subscription
1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Confirm cancellation. Your plan will not renew, and access remains active until the end of the paid billing period.

## Deleting a Workspace Permanently
- Only the **Workspace Owner** can delete a workspace.
- Navigate to **Workspace Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Workspace**.
- This permanently deletes all boards, cards, and uploaded attachments.
- **30-Day Recovery Grace Period**: If deleted by accident, the Workspace Owner can contact support within 30 days to request database restoration. After 30 days, data is permanently purged in compliance with GDPR.
