# Changing Subscription Plans & Upgrading / Downgrading

## Upgrading Your Plan (Free to Pro, or Pro to Team)
- Upgrades take effect immediately upon checkout.
- You will be billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle.
- New features (such as higher automation limits, unlimited boards, or SSO) unlock instantly.

## Downgrading Your Plan (Team to Pro, or Pro to Free)
- Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing cycle**.
- You maintain access to all tier features until the renewal date.
- **Important**: Before downgrading to Free, ensure your workspace has 3 or fewer active boards and 5 or fewer members. If limits are exceeded when the downgrade executes, older boards become read-only until archived.

## Adding or Removing Seats
- When adding a new member to a paid workspace, a prorated charge for that seat is calculated for the rest of the billing period.
- When removing a member, the empty seat license remains available for reassignment until the next renewal.
