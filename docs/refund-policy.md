# FlowBoard Refund Policy

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
FlowBoard offers a no-questions-asked 14-day money-back guarantee for first-time purchases of monthly and annual **Pro** and **Team** subscriptions.

## Monthly Subscriptions
- Refund requests submitted within **14 days** of the initial subscription charge are refunded in full.
- Renewals of monthly plans are non-refundable once the billing date has passed.

## Annual Subscriptions
- New annual subscriptions can be refunded within **14 days** of initial purchase.
- Annual renewal charges can receive a prorated refund if requested within **30 days** of the renewal date.
- After 30 days, annual subscriptions cannot be refunded, but you retain full access until the end of the paid term.

## Exceptions & Process
- Add-on seat purchases made accidentally can be refunded or credited within 7 business days.
- To request a refund, go to **Settings > Billing > Invoices** or submit a ticket with your invoice number. Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.
