# Data Export and Backup Instructions

FlowBoard allows workspace administrators to export boards and account data at any time.

## Board-Level Export (CSV & JSON)
1. Open the board you wish to export.
2. Click the **Board Menu (...)** in the top right corner.
3. Select **Export Board**.
4. Choose your format:
   - **CSV**: Best for spreadsheet analysis in Excel or Google Sheets (exports card titles, status, assignee, due date, tags, custom fields).
   - **JSON**: Full hierarchical export including card descriptions, comments, checklists, and attachment metadata.

## Full Workspace Backup (Pro & Team Plans)
1. Navigate to **Workspace Settings > Data Management**.
2. Click **Request Full Workspace Archive**.
3. Select whether to include file attachments (ZIP bundle).
4. An export link will be sent to the workspace Owner's email within 30 minutes. Download links remain active for 7 days.
